SECTION: Student Parking Permit Options and Fees
All motor vehicles parked on campus property must display a valid university parking permit or pay hourly visitor rates. Permits are available on an academic year or single semester basis.
• Commuter Student Permit (Yellow Lot): $175 per semester or $320 for the full academic year. Permits parking in Lots A, B, and the South Garage.
• Resident Student Permit (Red Lot): $240 per semester. Reserved for students living in campus residence halls; permits parking in Lots R1 through R4.
• Evening Only Permit: $90 per semester; valid in commuter lots after 4:30 PM on weekdays and all day on weekends.
Permits may be purchased online via the Parking Portal starting August 1.

SECTION: Campus Shuttle Schedule and Routes
The University operates two fare-free shuttle routes for students, faculty, and staff displaying a valid University ID card.
• Blue Line (Campus Circulator): Runs every 10 minutes between North Campus, Student Union, and Central Library Monday through Friday from 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM.
• Silver Line (Commuter Express): Connects the South Remote Parking Lots and the Main Quad every 15 minutes between 7:30 AM and 6:30 PM.
Live shuttle tracking is available through the Campus Go mobile application.

SECTION: Parking Violations and Appeals Procedure
Vehicles parked without a valid permit, parked outside designated lot tiers, or blocking fire lanes will be issued a parking citation. Standard parking fines range from $35 for an expired meter to $150 for unauthorized parking in an accessible space. Students wishing to contest a parking citation must file an online appeal within 14 calendar days of citation issuance through the Parking Portal. Unpaid citations are transferred to the student's bursar account after 30 days and will place a registration hold on the student account.

SECTION: Bicycle Storage and Campus Micromobility
Bicycles and electric scooters must be parked at outdoor bike racks and may not be brought inside residence halls or academic buildings. Free indoor secure bike cages are located at the North and South Transit Centers; access can be encoded onto your student ID card by visiting the Parking Office during normal business hours (Monday through Friday, 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM).
