SECTION: Clinic Hours and Walk-In Policy
The Student Health Center is located in Building 4 and provides routine medical care, preventive screenings, minor illness treatment, and travel medicine.
• Regular Clinic Hours: Monday through Thursday from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM; Friday from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
• Urgent Walk-In Care: Available without an appointment Monday through Friday between 8:30 AM and 11:30 AM for acute symptoms (e.g., high fever, sudden injury, asthma flare).
• Routine primary care visits should be scheduled in advance through the HealthyStudent portal or by calling 555-0143.

SECTION: Counseling and Psychological Services (CAPS)
CAPS offers free, confidential mental health services to all currently registered undergraduate and graduate students. Services include individual short-term counseling, group therapy workshops, crisis intervention, and medication evaluations. Students are entitled to up to 12 free individual therapy sessions per academic year. Initial drop-in triage assessments are conducted Monday through Friday from 1:00 PM to 3:30 PM.

SECTION: After-Hours Medical Advice and Crisis Line
For medical concerns when the clinic is closed, students can reach the 24/7 Registered Nurse Advice Line at 555-0199 free of charge. For psychological crises after business hours, call CAPS 24/7 crisis support line at 555-0198 or text HOME to 741741 to reach the Crisis Text Line. For life-threatening emergencies, immediately dial 911 or Campus Police at 555-0911.

SECTION: Required Immunizations and Health Insurance Waiver
All newly admitted students must submit certified immunization records via the health portal before the first day of classes. Mandatory requirements include 2 doses of MMR (Measles, Mumps, Rubella), Meningitis ACWY (for students under 22 living in residence halls), and proof of Tdap within the last 10 years. Students covered by an existing private health insurance plan that meets university standards can submit an annual insurance waiver by September 1 to waive the automatic $1,450 student health insurance plan fee.
