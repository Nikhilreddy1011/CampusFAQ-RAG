SECTION: Course Add and Drop Deadlines
Students may add or drop full-term courses via the Student Portal during the official Add/Drop Period. For the Fall semester, the deadline to add a new course without instructor permission is the 10th business day of classes (September 15 at 11:59 PM). The final deadline to drop a course without a 'W' (Withdrawal) notation on the academic transcript is the 15th business day of classes (September 22). Courses dropped prior to this deadline qualify for a 100% tuition refund.

SECTION: Course Withdrawal Policy
After the initial drop deadline has passed, students may withdraw from individual courses through the 10th week of instruction (November 3). A withdrawal results in a grade of 'W' on the student's official transcript. A grade of 'W' carries no grade point penalty and does not affect the student's GPA, but the course credits count toward attempted hours for financial aid Satisfactory Academic Progress. No tuition refunds are issued for course withdrawals submitted after the third week of classes.

SECTION: Course Overload and Minimum Credit Requirements
Undergraduate students must enroll in at least 12 credit hours per semester to maintain full-time student status, which is required for institutional scholarships and on-campus housing eligibility. The standard maximum course load is 18 credit hours. Students with a cumulative GPA of 3.3 or higher may petition their academic dean's office for an overload permit to enroll in up to 21 credits.

SECTION: Final Exam Regulations
Final examinations must be administered according to the published University Final Exam Schedule. Instructors are not permitted to reschedule exams or administer comprehensive finals during the final week of regular classes. If a student is scheduled for three or more examinations on the same calendar day, the student may request rescheduling of the middle exam by submitting the Conflict Form to the Registrar at least 14 days prior to the start of exam week.

SECTION: Graduation Application Deadlines
Students expecting to complete degree requirements must file an official Application for Graduation through the portal. The deadline for Spring graduation is February 1. The deadline for Fall graduation is October 1. A late filing fee of $50 applies to submissions received after these cutoff dates.
